### Added
- Canvases have a full-screen mode that hides everything but the drawing, and a read-only mode so a canvas can be presented without a stray click changing it.
- Calls now run on a self-hosted LiveKit SFU: better quality, many more people in a room, and TURN relay so restrictive networks still connect.
- Spatial audio — a floor-plan view of the call where dragging someone moves their voice in your own mix. Positions are yours alone; nobody else hears your arrangement.
- Push-to-talk (hold Space), room-wide force-mute for any participant, and a per-person "mute for me" that only affects your playback.
- Emoji reactions during a call, and live polls on the call overlay.
- Voice messages: record, preview and send from the composer, with waveform playback.
- The Garden — a private focus space you enter to stop being reachable. Entering turns on Do Not Disturb and starts a countdown or stopwatch focus timer.
- Link previews: URLs unfurl into cards in chat, in encrypted DMs and in docs. Preview images are proxied, so a linked site never sees your IP.
- Sign in with Google or GitHub.
- Self-service password reset by email, via Resend or SMTP.
- Granular notification control: per-channel modes, per-type switches, and Do Not Disturb either on demand or on a quiet-hours schedule. Plus native macOS notifications for the desktop app.
- Streaming mode privacy shield: blur sensitive content, reveal it in levels, and swap in temporary nicknames while you're on camera.
- Appearance preferences — theme presets, chat style, motion effects and remappable keyboard shortcuts.
- Disappearing messages and per-channel privacy settings.
- A home screen that shows where you left off and what moved while you were away.
- A peek panel for opening a doc, canvas or board without leaving the conversation.
- Public privacy policy and terms pages.

### Changed
- The canvas engine is now Excalidraw: a richer toolset, and no license key for self-hosters. **Canvases created before this release open empty** — the old engine's data can't be converted.
- The Garden is a private, single-player space. The shared multiplayer hub it started as has been removed.
- The whole app was rebuilt on a documented design system, so buttons, menus, modals and empty states look and behave the same everywhere.
- Settings are split into per-tab pages with a routed profile workspace.
- Scheduled meetings get a sharp-generated meet link you can copy or join in one click.
- Chat on phones is denser, with a composer that works the way you'd expect from a phone.

### Fixed
- Calls recover when the picture-in-picture window is torn down, and guests are named consistently everywhere they appear.
- The macOS desktop app is signed and notarized, so it opens without a Gatekeeper warning.
